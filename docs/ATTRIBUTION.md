**Carving, Ivory Putto** is from The Hunt Museum's public-domain 3D library. The sculpture mesh is normalized and small disconnected pieces were removed for the local generator. [Museum 3D library](https://www.huntmuseum.com/explore/3d-library/) · [Direct scan record](https://zenodo.org/records/21490316)

The Windows archive includes **llama.cpp b12651** and its included runtime license files in **atelier/engine**. **Qwen3 8B GGUF** is an optional separate download under its own Apache-2.0 terms. [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12651) · [Qwen3 8B GGUF](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B-GGUF)

The showcase was rendered in **Blender Cycles**. Blender, Rhino, and Qwen model weights are separate from this release.
