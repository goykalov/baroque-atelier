# Barocco Atelier roadmap

Barocco Atelier explores how a fixed room can support different Baroque interiors. The goal is an editable room with a coherent ceiling, wall rhythm, and ornament. The generator runs locally, with optional offline Qwen for composition search.

## Next public preview

| Priority | Work | How we will check it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Reproducible Windows and Rhino quickstart](https://github.com/goykalov/baroque-atelier/issues/2) | A fresh install can generate a room with local rules and rebuild a gallery recipe. |
| 1 | [Responsive Grasshopper preview](https://github.com/goykalov/baroque-atelier/issues/3) | Measure slider response and peak memory with the same dense recipe, then verify the final export. |
| 2 | [More varied architecture](https://github.com/goykalov/baroque-atelier/issues/4) | Compare wall rhythms, supports, and vaults inside a fixed room. Publish the raw views with the recipes. |

| 2 | [Relief that follows its supports](https://github.com/goykalov/baroque-atelier/issues/6) | Check attachment, clearances and configurable limits on relief thickness and projection. |
| 2 | [Reversible edits to one zone](https://github.com/goykalov/baroque-atelier/issues/7) | Compare a revision with its parent recipe and verify that untouched zones keep their geometry. |

## Further experiments

- Develop distinct regional composition rules from attributed architectural references. Compare room layouts, ornament hierarchy, and the way relief meets the building.
- Test whether an offline review pass can identify weak compositions and propose revisions that improve a matched pair of views.
- Study a small fabrication sample with sections and test pieces to inform the geometry constraints for ornamental plasterwork.
